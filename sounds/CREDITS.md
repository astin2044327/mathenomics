# Sound credits

Recordings come from Wikimedia Commons. Each clip was trimmed to a few seconds and loudness-normalised. Edited clips keep the licence of the original. Some hard-to-find animals use a closely related species or movement sound; those cases are noted.

- **Bee** (`sounds/bee.mp3`): [Bee buzzing sound (animal noises).opus](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bee_buzzing_sound_(animal_noises).opus) by Free Sounds Library User Spanac, CC BY 3.0
